# Vinyl Manufacturer: Bullmark (Modern)

(For information about the original company founded in the 1960s, see the [Bullmark (Vintage)](vinyl-manufacturer-bullmark-vintage.md) article.)

In 2009, the **Bullmark** brand was relaunched by Saburo Ishizuki. 

The company initially sold reissues of vintage Bullmark figures, but later added new vintage-style designs to their product lineup.

Bullmark’s products are currently available to purchase directly via their website and through several retailers throughout Japan.
